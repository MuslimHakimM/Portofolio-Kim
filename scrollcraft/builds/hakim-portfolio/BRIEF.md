## 2. The Eight Brief Topics
1. **Vibe & palette:** "Gelap modern / Obsidian" — matte architectural obsidian (`#0B0C0F`) and graphite surfaces (`#13151A`) with warm off-white ink (`#F2EFE9`), cool graphite secondary ink (`#9A9EA8`), and one locked Android-native emerald accent (`#3DDC84`), breaking into one hard-cut archival paper chapter (`#EFECE5`) with dark emerald (`#0B7A42`) for verified certificates. Zero AI-purple gradients, zero neon glow.
2. **Scroll journey:** Dimensional Obsidian Title Page (no splash intro) -> Quiet Profile & Education spread -> Engineered Peak: 4 Shipped Apps inspected through a sticky Android Build Inspector -> Hard-cut Archival Paper Credentials spread -> Precision Technical Stack ledger -> Resolved Correspondence Desk & Colophon.
3. **Energy curve:** Composed presence (Title) -> Quiet editorial restraint (Profile/Education, silence before peak) -> High visual immersion & tactile interaction (Selected Work peak) -> Crisp archival authority (Hard-cut light paper Credentials) -> Compact technical clarity (Stack) -> Calm, complete resolution (Correspondence).
4. **Feeling curve & Peak:** See sections 3 and 4 below.
5. **Signature move (delegated):** Sticky **Android Build Inspector** inside the Selected Work chapter. As the visitor scrolls past each project's editorial dossier, a precision anodized device stage morphs via a scroll-linked `clip-path` wipe from each app's real **Splash Screen** to its **Live Dashboard**, accompanied by scroll-drawn SVG technical callout traces (`stroke-dashoffset` driven from `--sc-p`) and interactive Splash / Dashboard / Dual inspection controls, while a floating chapter pill navbar tracks active & visited chapters.
6. **Aesthetic family:** Modern Obsidian Editorial (restraint, crisp hairlines, sharp type hierarchy, tactile hardware elevation).
7. **Structure:** Distinct editorial chapters (`Chaptered editorial` grammar) with hard ground cuts rather than one continuous flight.
8. **Real assets supplied:** `profile_hakim.png`, 4 apps × 3 real screenshots (`splash`, `dashboard`, `dual`), 5 verified certificates + Bangkit transcript (6 scans), CV PDF + 2 high-res page PNGs. Zero generated imagery required.
9. **User-requested navigation:** Floating pill navbar (`floating pill`) serving as an editorial chapter folio (active chapter indicator, visited ledger dots, quick `CV` trigger, and responsive mobile pill menu).

## 3. Feeling Curve (Emotion -> Visual Cause)
1. **Presence** — Asymmetric obsidian title page; portrait plate and overlapping foreground Android screen separate cleanly along independent `data-sc-parallax` planes.
2. **Grounding** *(authored quiet before the peak)* — Calm graphite editorial spread with Hakim's engineering bio, real verified figures (`3.54`, `900`, `5`) ticking in once via `data-sc-count`, and a restrained chronological education ledger.
3. **Immersion** *(THE PEAK)* — Deep obsidian studio ground; the sticky Android Build Inspector scrubs smoothly between Splash and Dashboard states via `--sc-p` clip-path curtains while SVG architecture callout lines draw themselves beside each real app dossier.
4. **Authority** — Hard cut from obsidian to warm archival paper (`#EFECE5`) via `data-sc-reveal` boundary wipe; verified Bangkit and Dicoding certificates sit as crisp tactile archival plates (`data-sc-tilt="5"`).
5. **Clarity** — Return to graphite obsidian; compact filterable technical specification ledger (18 verified skills in tabular rows, not bloated cards).
6. **Resolve** — Deep obsidian close that holds steadily on screen; direct channel colophon plus a live correspondence form that remembers if the visitor clicked "Discuss this project" from a case study.

## 5. Grammar, Fingerprint Gate & Score Table
- **Grammar:** `Chaptered editorial` (adapted cleanly with the user's requested floating pill chapter folio).
- **Why the other seven lost:** Filmic one-shot hides navigation and requires scrub video; Continuous world requires an expensive unbroken video chain; Live surface forbids editorial photography; Typographic poster forbids media; Gallery forbids narrative profile/education progression; Split stage forces binary comparison; Rhythmic cutlist bans editorial pacing and hold.
- **Bans enforced:** No continuous `drift`, no `scrub`, no `spotlight`, no `magnet`, no pinned crossfade type acts, no centered hero, no em dashes, no `01 / 06` section counters, no "scroll to explore" cues, no gradient text, no neon glow.
- **Fingerprint Gate:** Registry at `scrollcraft/FINGERPRINTS.md` starts empty (first build clears automatically).
